Kimono Drive · screen mockups · round 2

Three ways to build Drive

Each version is a different layout for the same product, drawn in Kata with Drive's own accent. They all follow the product rules on the first tab, and each one covers the whole feature list: uploading, browsing, selecting many items, renaming, moving, deleting and restoring, previews, public links, spaces with icons, people, and turning a folder into a space. Pick per version: keep / adjust / kill. You can also mix parts, for example the sidebar from A with the inspector from B.
