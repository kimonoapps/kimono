# ADR 0005: Type — Shippori Mincho with Zen Maru Gothic

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-31
- Sheet: `specimens/10-type.html`

## Context

Trying the phone app in Compose with Material 3 Expressive, the owner liked
how playful it felt and asked whether Kimono's type should change, while
keeping an identity of its own rather than borrowing Google's. Four pairs were
put on real screens: the current Shippori Mincho with Zen Kaku Gothic,
Shippori with Zen Maru Gothic, Kaisei Decol with Zen Maru, and Kaisei Decol
with Zen Kaku.

## Decision

Names and headings stay in **Shippori Mincho** (600). Everything read or
pressed moves from Zen Kaku Gothic to **Zen Maru Gothic** (400, 500, 700),
the rounded member of the same Zen family: warmer, matching the springy
motion, still plainly Kimono's.

It is cut everywhere at once: `--k-sans` in `packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css`
and the Portal's `@font-face` rules (`apps/portal/public/fonts`), the sign-in
page skin (`infra/compose/server/authentik/blueprints/kimono.css`, served from
`apps/portal/public/brand/fonts`), and the Compose app's theme (Latin subsets
in `apps/compose/shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/font`). Each weight is a
Latin cut of about 12 KB as woff2.

## Consequences

- Zen Kaku Gothic's files are removed; nothing references them.
- Kaisei Decol is ruled out (see `motifs.md`). Google's own faces are ruled out
  as a direction.
- The React Native app in `apps/mobile` still carries Zen Kaku; it is paused
  in favour of the Compose app and was not changed.
