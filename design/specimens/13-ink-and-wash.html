Specimen 13 · Ink and wash

The petal's brush, across Kimono

The ink-and-wash petal is a plum brush line that wobbles and breaks over a watercolour wash pooling at the rim. Here that hand goes everywhere Kimono draws something: app blooms, the icon tiles in lists, portraits, empty states, the loading mark and the sign-in art. Every mark is drawn slightly differently each time it renders, so nothing looks stamped.

The rule that keeps it from getting messy
  • Ink is for Kimono's art and marks: blooms, petals, crests, portraits, empty states, the sun.
  • Controls stay clean Material: seals, fields, Choice, cards and text are never brushed.
  • One brush colour: plum ink. Washes take the app's or the role's colour.
