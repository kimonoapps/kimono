08

What stays and what goes

Kata keeps

  • Shippori Mincho for names, Zen Maru Gothic for everything else
  • Suō, plum and moss as the only hues; moss still means running
  • Each app's bloom and the sakura crossing between apps
  • The seal: carved ring, base, sinking press and ink impression
  • Hold-to-confirm with petals for anything destructive
  • The joint switch and the shoji door transition
  • The sakura sun on the sign-in page

Goes

  • 1px hairline boxes as the way to group things
  • 0–2px corners
  • Paper grain and the near-identical beiges
  • Ema plaques, cords and tassels on Home
  • 12px as the default label size
  • The hairline box with a chevron on dropdowns
  • Authentik's default sign-in page
