What is here, all under the licence above:

- build_case.py: modified from the enclosure's enclosures/case-426/src/build.py. It
  builds the published 4.26" standard case (tub, lid with its side keys, bottom keys,
  the display as held) with one change: the back carries Kimono's mark (the white
  branch on a rose square), cut 0.4 mm deep and inlaid in two colours, in place of
  the Silkscreen book icon, and the Silkscreen wordmark is left off. Run it against a checkout of the enclosure at the commit above.
- make_model.py: makes silkscreen-kimono.glb from build_case.py's parts (colours,
  decimation for the web, a page on the screen).
- make_turntable.py: renders the GLB as the sprite sheet the Kimono phone app turns
  (apps/compose/shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/files/silkscreen/).
- silkscreen-kimono.glb: the model Kimono profiles show.

Not covered by the licence above:

- The Kimono name and the mark's artwork (kimono-mark.svg) are Kimono's marks, used
  to emboss the case. The licence covers the case design; it does not grant rights to
  the Kimono name or mark. Likewise the Silkscreen name and logo, which this case
  does not carry.
- The page on the screen is the opening of Jane Austen's Pride and Prejudice (1813),
  in the public domain, set in Shippori Mincho (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
